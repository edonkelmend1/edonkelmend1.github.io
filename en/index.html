Professional profile

Edon Kelmendi is a software engineer from Peja, Kosovo, with experience in application development and ICT teaching.

Edon Kelmendi was born on 4 January 2004 in Peja, where he completed primary school and then secondary school in the Natural Sciences track at the “Bedri Pejani” Gymnasium.

He completed a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science at AAB College (Prishtina) and is currently completing a Master of Science in Computer Science at AAB College (Prishtina) and a Master of Science in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at the University of Liverpool (England).

His professional experience covers teaching, organising work and developing digital solutions for managing information. He is skilled in task planning, data management and professional communication.
